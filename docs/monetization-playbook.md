# Monetization Playbook - m365-copilot-adoption-command-center

This is a commercial planning artifact, not a revenue guarantee. It exists to turn an archived technical proof into a clear, ethical selling motion.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption and value command center
- **Primary buyer:** CIO offices, digital workplace teams, adoption leads, and Microsoft-focused consultancies.
- **Why this can sell:** Copilot ROI depends on use-case adoption, governance, enablement, and value tracking after licenses are purchased.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Copilot adoption audit** - $7k-$25k. Assess current license utilization, use-case mix, and governance gaps.
2. **Command-center pilot** - $30k-$100k. Deploy dashboards, adoption cohorts, and value rituals for one business unit.
3. **Adoption office retainer** - $8k-$35k/month. Run monthly value reviews, enablement updates, and governance scorecards.

## Acquisition Channels

- Microsoft partner ecosystem
- CIO adoption scorecard content
- Department-specific value-case pages

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Synthetic or anonymized data stays clear
- Dashboard metrics are tied to actions
- Governance and enablement are both represented

## Risk Boundaries

- Do not claim Microsoft endorsement
- Avoid ROI guarantees before baseline data
- Protect tenant and employee telemetry

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish a Copilot value scorecard
- **60 days:** Create a department adoption demo
- **90 days:** Offer the audit as the paid front door

## Metrics That Matter

- Audit starts
- License activation lift
- Use-case adoption
- Monthly retained accounts

## Positioning Note

Keep the repository's archived status visible. Sell the reusable workflow, diagnostic, enablement material, or pilot path that the proof makes credible; do not sell the archive as an unsupported production service.
